THE IDEA IN THREE STEPS

How WakeTrace follows a possible spill

1
Look for a possible slick

Radar images can show unusually dark patches on the sea. A dark patch is only a clue; it may have other causes.

2
Estimate where and when it may have started

Wind and ocean currents help the model work backward and show a possible origin area and time window.

3
Review nearby vessel reports

WakeTrace compares available ship-position reports with that area and time, then shows possible leads for a person to check.

The practice button uses generated data to demonstrate the steps. It does not discover a real spill or identify a responsible vessel.
