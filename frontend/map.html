What do the map symbols and terms mean?

Amber outline: a possible dark radar feature selected for review. It is not proof of oil.

Teal dashed area: a possible origin area estimated by the drift model. Its size shows uncertainty.

Blue dashed line: a vessel route to compare with the time and area. It is a lead, not an accusation.

Pink point: in practice mode, the invented release point used to check whether the exercise works.

Teal center: the planted center of the practice patch, not a measured oil location.

Coordinates: choose an area to search. GeoJSON positions are written longitude first, then latitude; coordinates do not tell WakeTrace where a spill happened.

Radar image: grayscale radar brightness, not a normal camera photo. Some dark areas can look like oil but have other causes.

AIS: ship-position messages. Coverage can be incomplete, and some results are approximate daily areas.
