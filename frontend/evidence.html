Plain-language guide to this page

UTC: one shared world time standard, so satellite and ship times can be compared fairly.

Release search window: the period the model checked backward from the image time. It is not a confirmed spill time.

Ensemble / particles: many slightly varied model paths. Their spread shows how uncertain the origin estimate is.

Dark-feature score: a screening priority based on image appearance, not the chance that the patch is oil.

AIS candidate score: a sorting aid based on the available position data, not a probability of guilt.

Input sources and settings: records of which data and model choices produced the displayed result, for review and replay.

Radar brightness (dB): how strongly the radar signal returned from the sea surface. It does not identify oil by itself.
